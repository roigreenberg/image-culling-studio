❓ ImageCulling Studio — Help & Shortcuts

4 Culling Views

⊞ Grid (G)

Browse your filtered shoot at high speed. Check multiple cards for batch rating/labeling, or click any photo to open Loupe.

📚 Burst Rows (M)

Review chronologically one burst/scenario per row. Press Space to expand all frames inline, or Enter to open Winner vs. Challenger.

▣ Loupe (E)

Inspect single frames at full camera resolution. Use Z for AF focus zoom, F for AF focus box, H for burned highlights, and hold Q to peek full resolution.

◫ Winner vs. Challenger (C)

Pinned Winner stays on the left while you step Challengers (←/→) on the right with synced zoom & drag pan. Press S to Swap Winner, K to Keep Both, and ↑/↓ to jump bursts (including single photos).

How AI Culling Scores Your Shoot

  • Project Kestrel Core: MegaDetector + SAM-HQ segments the wildlife subject, BioCLIP 2 identifies bird/animal species, and segmented sharpness scores subject/head focus independently of background bokeh.
  • SigLIP 2 Visual Scoring: Evaluates wildlife action/pose, eye catchlight, eye contact, occlusion, and golden-hour / sunrise / sunset landscape quality.
  • Two-Track Routing + Hybrid Light: Routes photos into Wildlife or Landscape, awarding a +15% Hybrid Light bonus when wildlife is photographed in dramatic sunrise/sunset light.
  • Manual Burst Split & Merge: Split a burst at any frame (S in Loupe or ✂ Split Here in Winner vs. Challenger). Splits are persisted in SQLite and automatically reapplied across fast re-selects.
  • Personal Ranker (🧠 Insights): Train a local LightGBM model from your own ratings/color labels so future re-selects and shoots adapt to your personal taste.

Safe Lightroom & Photo Mechanic XMP Sync

  • Develop Edits Preserved: Real-time XMP sync writes atomically and only updates xmp:Rating, xmp:Label, and photomechanic:ColorClass. All Lightroom Develop sliders, crops, and masks stay untouched.
  • Star Ratings Protected: Existing in-camera or Lightroom star ratings are never overwritten unless you explicitly rate a photo.
  • Undo & Restore: Press Ctrl+Z or open 📜 Logs & Restore to roll back any change (including its XMP snapshot).
